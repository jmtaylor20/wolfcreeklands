Common Questions

The things landowners ask most before starting a clearing, road, drainage, or site development project.

How much does land clearing cost?

Cost depends on acreage, brush density, tree size, access, and how clean you want the finished property. Every job is different, so the best next step is a free estimate.

Do you charge for an estimate?

No. Estimates are free. Call or text 334-207-3331, or send your project details through the form and we will follow up.

How far do you travel?

Wolf Creek Farms is based in Notasulga and works across Macon, Lee, Tallapoosa, Elmore, and Montgomery counties, including Auburn, Opelika, Tallassee, Lake Martin, Wetumpka, and Pike Road.

Do you take on small jobs?

Yes. A single driveway repair, one house pad, or a few acres of clearing are all normal jobs for us alongside larger land development work.

Can you fix standing water and washouts?

Yes. Drainage work, ditch shaping, culvert repair, and rip rap can move water off the property before it causes erosion or damage.

How soon can you start?

Scheduling depends on weather, ground conditions, and current jobs. Reach out early, especially for wet-season work, and we will give you a realistic window.
